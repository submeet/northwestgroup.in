About Northwest Group

Northwest Group is a leading supplier of raw materials to the tyre, rubber, and conveyor belt industries. Since our inception in 2002, we have grown from a startup with USD 1 million turnover to a company with USD 115 million in annual revenue by 2022.

Our success is built on strong relationships with manufacturers globally and a commitment to quality and reliability in every transaction.

Our Mission

To be a trusted partner in the supply of raw materials and specialized chemicals for the rubber and tyre industries, delivering consistent quality, competitive pricing, and exceptional service to customers across India and globally.

Our Vision

To be the most trusted and preferred partner, bridging the gap between global manufacturers and customers by providing seamless, innovative, and reliable solutions that drive mutual success.

Key Strengths

  • Experience: Over two decades of industry expertise
  • Global Network: Representation of top manufacturers worldwide
  • Product Range: Comprehensive portfolio of raw materials and specialty chemicals
  • Market Coverage: Strong presence in both established and emerging markets
  • Reliability: Consistent growth and customer satisfaction
  • Quality Assurance: Strict quality control and compliance standards

Primary Products & Services

  • Steel Cords for tyre reinforcement
  • Rubber Process Oils (GTL and conventional)
  • Rubber Chemicals (accelerators, activators, antioxidants)
  • Reinforcement Fibres
  • Specialty Chemicals for tyre and rubber manufacturing
  • Market promotion and supplier representation

Market Presence

Domestic Market (India)

Strong presence serving leading tyre manufacturers, rubber processors, and conveyor belt producers across India through our Northwest Enterprises division.

International Market

Global representation and supply capabilities through our Northwest International division, serving manufacturers and distributors across multiple countries.

Business Growth

  • 2002: Company inception with USD 1 million turnover
  • 2010-2015: Expansion into international markets
  • 2015-2020: Portfolio expansion and additional supplier representations
  • 2020-2022: Achieved USD 115 million turnover milestone
  • 2023-Present: Continued expansion and market diversification
