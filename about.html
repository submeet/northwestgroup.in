About us

Northwest Group
(2002-2025)
  • Commenced its operations in the year 2002.
  • Primarily engaged in supply of Raw Materials to Tyre, Rubber & Conveyor belt industry in India as well as abroad.
  • Assisting suppliers in rapid product promotion in well established as well as nascent markets.
  • Consistent turnover growth since the company's inception in 2002. (From USD 1 million in 2002 to 115 million by the end 2022).
  • Representing some of top manufacturers across the globe for Steel Cords, Rubber Process oils/GTL, Rubber Chemicals, Reinforcement Fibres, Speciality chemicals etc.
