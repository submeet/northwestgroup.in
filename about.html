Company Overview

Northwest Group is a leading supplier of raw materials to the tire, rubber, and conveyor belt industries across India and globally. Since our establishment in 2002, we have grown from a small enterprise to a major player in the industry.

We specialize in supplying high-quality raw materials including steel cords, rubber process oils, rubber chemicals, reinforcement fibers, and specialty chemicals. Our commitment to excellence and customer satisfaction has made us a trusted partner for businesses worldwide.

Our Mission & Vision

🎯 Our Mission

To be a trusted facilitator of premium raw materials, delivering exceptional value to our customers through innovation, reliability, and sustainable business practices. We are committed to supporting the growth of the tire and rubber industry worldwide.

🌟 Our Vision

To become the leading global supplier of raw materials for the tire and rubber industry, recognized for our quality, innovation, and customer-centric approach. We envision a future where our partnerships contribute to sustainable industrial growth.
