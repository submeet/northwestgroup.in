Northwest Group

(2002-2026)
  • Commenced its operations in the year 2002 as a dedicated facilitator in the tire and rubber industry.
  • Primarily engaged in the supply of raw materials to the Tyre, Rubber & Conveyor belt industry across India and globally.
  • Assisting international suppliers in rapid product promotion in well-established as well as nascent markets.
  • Consistent turnover growth since inception in 2002 — from USD 1 million in 2002 to USD 115 million by 2025, reflecting our market leadership and strategic partnerships.
  • Representing top-tier global manufacturers for Steel Cords, Rubber Process oils/GTL, Rubber Chemicals, Reinforcement Fibres, Specialty chemicals, and emerging materials.
  • Committed to excellence, innovation, and sustainable growth in the global tire and rubber industry through strategic partnerships and customer-centric solutions.
